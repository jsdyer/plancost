    How totals and break-even work

    Service returns to the regular price after the promotion. Equipment stops after its entered term. Setup and exit costs enter month one. An optional buyout enters the last comparison month. For a financed device, include any balance you would actually owe if ending early.

    Enter service and equipment before tax, or use tax-inclusive amounts with a zero tax rate. The calculator rounds each bill’s tax to the nearest cent. Credits and exit costs are entered after tax. A credit larger than the bill is carried as savings in the comparison. Break-even compares Offer A with the lowest-total-cost alternative and shows when that alternative keeps a cumulative lead through the final month. The monthly table compares that pair. CSV includes every offer in entry order.

    Your offers stay in this browser. Saving is optional. The shared CoolCode counter receives the tool ID.
